# Open Ballot — Subprocessors

This document lists the third-party subprocessors that Open Ballot may use to
process personal data on its behalf. Open Ballot enters into data processing
agreements with each subprocessor that provide at least the same level of
protection as required by applicable data protection law.

## Category: Platform hosting

| Subprocessor | Location | Purpose | Data Processing Agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Render (render.com) | United States | Web application hosting | Available on request |
| Neon (neon.tech) | United States | PostgreSQL database hosting | Available on request |

## Category: Email delivery

| Subprocessor | Location | Purpose | Data Processing Agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| SMTP provider (configurable) | Varies | Transactional email (verification, password reset, notifications) | Available on request |

## Category: Error reporting

| Subprocessor | Location | Purpose | Data Processing Agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sentry (configurable via SENTRY_DSN) | Varies | Error and exception reporting | Available on request |

## Subprocessors not used

Open Ballot does NOT use:
- Analytics providers (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
- Advertising networks
- Social media trackers
- AI model providers for training on user data

Changes to this list will be posted at https://open-ballot.onrender.com/privacy
